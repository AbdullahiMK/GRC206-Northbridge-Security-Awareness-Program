GRC206 – Designing a Successful Security Awareness Program
Project Overview
This repository contains my GRC206 individual assignment on designing a realistic 12-month Security Awareness Program for Northbridge Logistics, a fictional organisation with 800 employees working across its head office, warehouses and remote sales operations.
The programme is designed to address human-related cybersecurity risks, improve employee security behaviour and reduce security incidents within 12 months. It also considers the organisation's limited security resources and training budget.
Assignment Objectives
The main objectives of this assignment are to:
•	Identify key human-related security risks affecting Northbridge Logistics.
•	Segment employees into relevant audiences and identify their awareness needs.
•	Develop measurable security awareness objectives.
•	Design practical, role-based awareness and training activities.
•	Establish behavioural performance indicators to evaluate programme effectiveness.
•	Develop a 12-month programme calendar.
•	Explain how the programme will be reviewed and continuously improved.
Programme Framework
The programme follows the five-stage security awareness lifecycle:
1.	Assess: Identify human-related security risks, understand employee groups and establish baseline performance.
2.	Plan: Define programme objectives, measurable targets, responsibilities and role-based learning activities.
3.	Deliver: Implement awareness activities through briefings, micro-learning, phishing simulations, toolbox talks and other existing communication channels.
4.	Measure: Evaluate training participation, employee behaviour, reporting rates and security incident trends.
5.	Improve: Review results, collect feedback, update learning materials and implement corrective actions.
Priority Security Risks
The programme focuses on three priority risks identified in the assignment scenario:
•	Invoice and payment fraud: Employees may fall victim to fraudulent invoices or unauthorised supplier bank-detail changes.
•	Password reuse and account compromise: Reusing passwords after a breach can expose additional organisational accounts.
•	Mishandling regulated customer data: Inappropriate access, sharing or storage of customer information may lead to data exposure and regulatory consequences.
Target Audiences
The programme includes role-based awareness activities for:
•	Finance, procurement and accounts payable teams.
•	Warehouse and site employees.
•	Remote sales and customer-facing employees.
•	Head-office employees and managers.
•	IT administrators and privileged users.

Expected Programme Outcomes
The proposed programme aims to:
•	Reduce employee susceptibility to invoice-fraud attempts.
•	Improve password security and reporting of suspicious messages.
•	Encourage safer handling of regulated customer information.
•	Improve employee participation in security awareness activities.
•	Establish a continuous process for measuring and improving security behaviour.
All performance targets in the report are proposed programme targets. They are not actual Northbridge Logistics performance results.
Scenario Facts and Assumptions
The assignment scenario establishes that Northbridge Logistics has 800 employees, has experienced invoice-fraud attempts, has two employees who reused passwords after a breach, handles regulated customer data, and operates with limited security resources.
Additional operational arrangements and implementation details in the report are clearly treated as assumptions or proposals to be validated during the initial assessment.
